## 📖 Overview

**WorldView-Bench** is a benchmark designed to evaluate **Global Cultural Inclusivity (GCI)** in Large Language Models (LLMs). Rather than relying on multiple-choice or closed-form evaluation, WorldView-Bench measures how well language models acknowledge, balance, and integrate diverse cultural perspectives through **free-form generation**.

The benchmark is inspired by the **Multiplex Worldview** framework, which promotes the representation of multiple civilizational perspectives instead of reinforcing a single dominant worldview.

## ✨ Highlights

- 🌍 First benchmark specifically designed for evaluating **Global Cultural Inclusivity**
- 🧠 Measures cultural perspectives using **free-form generation**
- 🤖 Supports evaluation of both single-agent and multi-agent LLM systems
- 📊 Introduces quantitative analysis of **Perspective Distribution Score (PDS)** and Cultural Polarization
- 🌐 Designed for multilingual and cross-cultural benchmarking

---

## 📊 Benchmark Domains

The benchmark consists of **175 carefully designed prompts** spanning seven worldview domains.

| Domain | Questions |
|---------|----------:|
| Ethical / Moral | 25 |
| Religious | 25 |
| Lifestyle | 25 |
| Cultural Norms | 25 |
| Traditions | 25 |
| History | 25 |
| Technology | 25 |

**Total:** **175 benchmark questions**
